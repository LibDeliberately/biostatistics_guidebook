# Telling Correlation from Causation

When two variables move together, it is easy to talk as if one **caused** the other. This guide is how I keep **association** and **cause** in their own lanes—and when study design lets me take a causal step.

A significant bivariate test is not, by itself, a causal claim. For choosing the test, see [bivariate-hypothesis-test-guide.md](bivariate-hypothesis-test-guide.md).
