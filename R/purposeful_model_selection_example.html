  • Hours of sleep each night (p = 0.346)

Step 5: Check for interactions

Variables with clinical plausibility to interact with daily sodium intake.

Age: salt sensitivity increases with age

BMI: BMI is associated with sodium intake

Sex: Hormone prevalence may impact salt’s effect on vascular health

Race: Cultural differences in dietary sources may impact overall intake, and there are genetic differences in salt sensitivity between some racial groups.

Smoking Status: Smoking alters tastes preferences & increases salt cravings
